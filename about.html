About Eclipse

Our story, our mission

Eclipse was born from a simple idea: that fashion should not be constrained by labels, seasons, or noise. We are an Algerian brand redefining streetwear through a lens of stark minimalism.

In a world of constant visual overload, we find power in simplicity. Our monochrome palette is a deliberate choice—black and white are the foundation of style, offering endless versatility and a timeless aesthetic.

We design for everyone. The Eclipse silhouette is inherently unisex, stripping away traditional boundaries to create clothing that focuses purely on form, fit, and comfort. Every piece is meticulously crafted in Algeria, reflecting our commitment to local talent and premium quality.

Minimalism

Clean lines, essential forms. We remove the unnecessary.

Quality

Premium fabrics designed to endure both time and trends.
